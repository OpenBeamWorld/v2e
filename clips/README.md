# Real clips

Planned: manifests for openly licensed or self-filmed real clips, one or more per family (crash,
rollover or trip, drop or bounce), each with independent truth such as a survey, a speed trap or
a published test report. A manifest records the clip's licence, its sha256, the truth and its
source, and a fetch script. Only CC0, CC BY or public-domain clips, and clips we film with
releases, are accepted.
